About Us

Doctors Without Borders (MSF) was founded in 1971 in France by doctors and journalists.

Our Mission: To provide impartial medical care to those who need it most, regardless of race, religion, or politics.

We won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1999 for our humanitarian work.

Our Principles

  • Independence
  • Impartiality
  • Neutrality
